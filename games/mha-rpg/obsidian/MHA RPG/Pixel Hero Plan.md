# Pixel Hero Plan (Pixel Hero Card, then Layers)

> **Update 2026-11-28 (Bailey):** the characters must look **exactly** as in his character sheet. Nothing is redrawn or changed.
> The 16 characters (8 bodies, bare and in costume) are saved pixel-exact in the repo (`games/style-guide/characters/`, 0 differing pixels).
> My own hand-drawn hero card is parked. Grid size 128x192 and the palette check were done before this change.

*From the idea-refine session. Decided by Bailey.*

## Problem Statement
How might we get one MHA-style pixel hero that looks right to you, in your own pixel style, without needing a huge game first?

## What Bailey chose
- **Game:** MHA-style superhero RPG · **For:** just Bailey · **Success (2 weeks):** one character that looks right · **Style:** pixel art
- **Direction C:** hero card first, then layers (body, then hair and clothes on top).

## Recommended Direction
Do the **hero card first**: one front-facing hero, hand-placed pixel by pixel on a grid of about 64x96, with shaded tones, a dark outline and warm light with cool shadows, as in the muscular-hero reference ([[Style Guide]]). Bailey judges it side by side with that picture.

If it looks right, it becomes the **body layer**; hair and clothes become separate layers on top (body first, clothes second). If not, change the method after one picture.

## Key Assumptions to Validate
- [ ] Hand-placing pixels can reach the detail of the reference. *Test: one hero card, compared side by side.*
- [ ] A body drawn once can accept hair and clothes as layers. *Test: add one hair layer and one shirt layer.*
- [ ] Bailey would rather judge a picture than fix a game. *Test: his reaction to the first card.*

## MVP Scope
**In:** one front-facing male hero, bare body in shorts (like the reference), shaded, outlined, on a flat background. **Out:** everything else.

## Not Doing (and Why)
- **3D or Blender anything:** it cost hours and gave the wrong look (files removed, see [[MHA RPG - Decisions]]).
- **Animation and walking:** the card has to look right first.
- **Enemies, maps, a city:** after the hero works.
- **AI image generators:** Bailey's rule, and it must be original.
