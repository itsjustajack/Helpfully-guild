# Style Guide

Full text and the three reference pictures live in the repo: `games/style-guide/STYLE_GUIDE.md` and `games/style-guide/references/`.

**References:** a muscular pixel hero on a dusk street · a daytime pixel city street · the painted isometric records office · **a character-creator sheet: 8 body types, bare and in costume** (the layering target: base body, then hair and costume on top, fitted per body).

**Rules in short**
1. One pixel grid: character and background share the same pixel size.
2. Hard pixels only: no blur, no smooth gradients.
3. Limited palette with 3-5 tone ramps per colour.
4. Warm highlights, cool shadows (hue-shifted shading).
5. Dither for gradients.
6. Dark outline on characters.
7. Body first, clothes second (separate layers).
8. Judge side by side with the references, big and clear.

Inside any one picture, everything shares one style.

## HD-2D rules (Bailey, 2026-11-28)
Source: his HD-2D note ("tattoos" in it was a voice-to-text slip for "photos", meaning the reference screenshots; no tattoos).
1. **Clean line work.** Crisp, continuous edges; no fuzzy or broken lines.
2. **Sharp silhouette.** The outline shape reads first, even as a flat black shape.
3. **Flat shading, minimal dithering.** Hard tone steps, no dotted gradients (sky bands are hard bands now).
4. **Distinct pixel clusters.** Group pixels into readable clumps (leaves, bricks, windows), not noise.
Test rule: try a new detail on minimal clothing / a plain body first, then add complex gear.
Palette is no longer strictly limited (Bailey: break that rule if something else helps).
