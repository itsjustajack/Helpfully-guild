# Systems Spec (summary of Bailey's PDF)

*Summary only — the PDF is the source.*

- **Hardware vs Software:** strong Quirks strain the body; heat gauge, short-circuit, skeletal strain.
- **Quirk types:** Emitter (energy/stamina, AoE), Transformation (stance, stamina drain), Mutant (passive, fixed hitbox), Composite (unstable).
- **Law and society:** Pro Hero licensing history, HPSC covert side.
- **Support gear:** assistive vs tactical; recoil suppressor, vector controller, mobility enhancer, exoskeleton.
- **Look:** thick ink lines, cel shading, 3D onomatopoeia, destructible environments, comic-panel HUD near the character.
- **Lessons from past MHA games:** no auto-combo mashing, no gacha or power creep, no stun-lock; fair, earnable progression.
- **Reputation:** Public Trust, HPSC sanction, "Stain factor".
