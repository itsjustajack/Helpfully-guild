# Design Concept (summary of Bailey's PDF)

*Summary only — the PDF is the source.*

- **Skyrim-style sandbox**, "use to improve" progression, set in superhero Japan.
- **Regions:** Musutafu & U.A., Hosu City, Kamino Ward, Deika City, underground sewers.
- **Factions:** HPSC, Pro Hero agencies, support gear industry, villain underworld.
- **Original character creation:** body, mutant traits, voice, background origin (Prodigy, Late-Bloomer, Underground Brawler, Support-Track Transfer), hero/villain/vigilante name.
- **Six gear slots:** undersuit, armor plating, gauntlets/bracers, footwear, headgear, utility; gear takes battle damage.
- **Quirks:** Emitter, Transformation, Mutant, plus Quirkless + gear; each with a body drawback and gear synergy.
- **Growth:** Power Output, Drawback Tolerance, Fine Control; crisis-triggered Quirk Awakenings.
- **Three paths:** Hero student, Villain, Vigilante.
- **World systems:** public Quirk law, collateral damage meter, crime and crisis events, up to 3 companions with combo synergies.
